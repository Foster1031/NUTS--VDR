The Story Behind NUTS!

This project is also serving as my capstone for an MIT Executive Education course, Applied Agentic AI for Organizational Transformation. I'm using the course and capstone project as a ground-up development of a new business — covering research, product design and placement, operations management, marketing, finance, and KPI development, alongside the use of AI agents and technology to build this site and all the materials in it. It's been a fun and informative project that's taught me a great deal about the practical functionality of advanced AI systems. I thought it would also be great if it had a philanthropic purpose — hence the game and its small philanthropic goal.

Nearly thirty years ago, a retired Marine Corps Colonel told me the story of Brigadier General Anthony McAuliffe, who commanded the besieged 101st Airborne Division at Bastogne during the Battle of the Bulge. When German forces demanded his surrender in December 1944, McAuliffe's entire reply was a single word: “Nuts!” The 101st held the line — and the story stuck with me ever since as a lesson in never giving up, which is exactly why it felt like the right name for this project. Adding documents

Easiest: in "Add document," click the upload box and pick a file — it's stored right here on the site and everyone can download it directly. Good for files under 20MB.

For bigger files (long videos, huge archives), link out to Google Drive or Dropbox instead. Two minutes, tops.

📗 Google Drive

  1. Upload your file to Drive (drag it into drive.google.com).
  2. Right-click the file → Share.
  3. Under "General access," change Restricted to Anyone with the link (leave as Viewer).
  4. Click Copy link, then paste it into "Add document" here.

📘 Dropbox

  1. Upload your file at dropbox.com.
  2. Hover over the file → click Share (or Copy link).
  3. Make sure the link setting is Anyone with this link can view.
  4. Copy the link and paste it into "Add document" here.

Tip: name files clearly (e.g. NUTS-budget-v3.xlsx) so the list stays tidy.
